KMTakip 11 – Widget

Home-screen widget added to the KMTakip 10 project.

Widget shows:
- Current tracking state
- Distance
- Speed
- Elapsed time

Actions:
- Start when idle
- Pause / Resume while tracking
- Stop while tracking
- Tap title/stats to open the app

Widget reads the existing km_tracking SharedPreferences used by TrackingService.
It is refreshed by the foreground service, and the existing notification/tracking logic is preserved.
